# Curated palmistry knowledge base (traditional / cultural claims only - NOT scientific)
# Each "## " block is one retrievable chunk. Edit/extend this file to improve the specialisation.

## heart_line overview
Traditional palmistry reads the heart line as the crease running across the upper palm below the fingers. It is associated with emotional style, affection and how a person relates to others. It is a cultural interpretation, not a fact.

## heart_line long deep curved
A long, deep, gently curved heart line is traditionally linked with warmth, expressiveness and openness in relationships.

## heart_line short straight faint
A short or straight heart line is traditionally linked with a reserved, practical or private approach to emotions. A faint line is read as a quieter emotional expression.

## head_line overview
The head line crosses the middle of the palm. Tradition links it with thinking style, learning, focus and decision making.

## head_line long straight
A long, straight head line is traditionally read as logical, analytical and detail-oriented thinking.

## head_line curved sloping
A curved or downward-sloping head line is traditionally read as imaginative, creative and intuitive thinking.

## head_line short faint
A short head line is traditionally read as direct, quick, practical thinking. A faint one as a more flexible, less fixed outlook.

## life_line overview
The life line curves around the base of the thumb. Tradition links it with vitality, energy, life changes and general enthusiasm for life. It is NOT a measure of lifespan or health.

## life_line long deep
A long, deep life line is traditionally associated with stamina, resilience and a steady energy level.

## life_line short faint
A short or faint life line is traditionally said to suggest a more changeable energy level or preference for flexibility. Palmists stress that it does not indicate a short life.

## life_line curved wide
A wide, sweeping curve is traditionally linked with enthusiasm and an active outlook; a tight curve with a more careful, conserving nature.

## fate_line overview
The fate line (or career line) runs vertically up the centre of the palm. Many people do not have one. Tradition links it with career path, direction and sense of purpose. Absence is considered normal and is not a negative sign.

## fate_line present deep
A clear fate line is traditionally read as a strong sense of direction or a focused career path.

## fate_line absent
When no fate line is visible, tradition reads this as a flexible, self-directed path with fewer fixed expectations. This is a very common situation.

## career themes
Palmistry traditionally combines the head line (thinking style) and fate line (direction) to describe career themes: a long straight head line with a clear fate line suggests structured, analytical work; a curved head line suggests creative work; a missing fate line suggests adaptable, varied work. Entertainment only.

## financial themes
Traditionally, financial themes are linked to the clarity of the fate line, the strength of the head line (planning) and general line depth (steady effort). These are cultural symbolism only and must never be used for financial decisions.

## relationship themes
Relationship themes are traditionally read mainly from the heart line, with the head line indicating how emotion and reason balance. Entertainment only.

## personality themes
Personality in palmistry is described by combining the heart line (emotional style), head line (thinking style) and life line (energy). Hand shape adds a four-element typology.

## hand shape earth
Earth-type hands (square palm, shorter fingers) are traditionally described as practical, grounded and reliable.

## hand shape air
Air-type hands (square palm, longer fingers) are traditionally described as curious, communicative and analytical.

## hand shape water
Water-type hands (rectangular palm, longer fingers) are traditionally described as sensitive, intuitive and empathetic.

## hand shape fire
Fire-type hands (rectangular palm, shorter fingers) are traditionally described as energetic, spontaneous and confident.

## left versus right hand
A common tradition says the non-dominant hand shows inherited or natural tendencies and the dominant hand shows how those tendencies developed through life choices. Differences between hands are read as change over time. Not scientific.

## future narratives
Traditional readers phrase future themes as possibilities, e.g. "periods of change" or "opportunities to build focus". Palmistry cannot predict events; narratives must be framed as symbolic and entertaining.

## uncertainty and limits
Line detection from a photograph depends on lighting, focus, skin texture and camera angle. Faint or uncertain detections should be stated as uncertain, and absent features must not be invented.
